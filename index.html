Module 01

Understanding the Soft Malay Wedding Look

Tutorial Video — Module 1Step-by-step walkthrough
Watch on YouTube ↗

Important Notes

The Soft Malay Wedding style is about creating a wedding photo that feels clean, soft, bright, romantic, and natural. The photo should look elegant without feeling too heavy, too contrasty, or over-edited.

Final image should have a bright and airy feel, especially for Malay wedding scenes with white outfits, pelamin decoration, floral background, and soft indoor lighting.

White Balance

Photo should not look too yellow, too blue, or too green.

Skin Tone

Face should look soft and natural. Avoid too orange, too red, or too pale.

Highlight Control

Malay wedding photos often have white baju, veil, bright pelamin. Make sure white areas still keep detail.

Green Control

Pelamin decoration has many green leaves. Green should support the subject, not overpower.

Basic installation steps

  1. Open Lightroom Classic
  2. Go to Develop module
  3. Find Presets panel
  4. Click + icon
  5. Choose Import Presets
  6. Select the preset file
  7. Click Import
  8. Check if preset appears in list

If your preset comes as DNG

Common Editing Problems
& Quick Fixes

Tutorial Part 1
Watch ↗
Tutorial Part 2
Watch ↗

Photo Too Orange

Cause: Temp too high. Fix: Reduce temp, reduce orange saturation.

Skin Too Red

Cause: Tint too magenta. Fix: Tint toward green, reduce red saturation.

Face Too Dark

Cause: Backlight. Fix: Increase exposure, lift shadows, use masking.

White Baju Overexposed

Cause: Exposure too high. Fix: Reduce highlights, whites, exposure slightly.

Recommended export settings

  • File Format: JPG
  • Color Space: sRGB
  • Quality: 100
  • Resize: Long Edge 3000-4000px
  • Resolution: 240-300ppi
  • Sharpening: Standard for Screen
